## Single-link failures

The 20 busiest backbone links (tier-1 and tier-2 internal) are cut one at a time, each given 8 s to settle and then restored. IGP time is from the cut to the last FIB change it caused, so it includes the 150 ms failure detection. Broken flow-seconds counts traffic that was looping, black-holed or unroutable while the network caught up.

| routing | IGP median | IGP p90 | IGP max | peak loss (mean) | broken flow-s (total) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| OSPF (link-state) | 277 ms | 319 ms | 323 ms | 5.67% | 23934.1 |
| RIP (distance-vector) | 1429 ms | 2050 ms | 2267 ms | 4.99% | 26816.0 |
| Q-routing (RL) | n/a | n/a | n/a | 6.79% | 25693.9 |

Q-routing has no discrete convergence event (it adapts every tick), so only the damage columns apply to it.
